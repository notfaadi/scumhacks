01 — The kit

Four reads. One overlay.

Each module is a layer on the SCUM window. Turn on the ones you want. Leave the rest off.

01

Player ESP

Names, distance, and health on prisoners through cover, so a tree line is a list instead of a guess. Built for roads, bases, and the long walk between them.

02

Loot ESP

Guns, ammo, and medical supplies marked in the world. Empty houses stay empty.

03

Aimbot

Optional sight assistance toward a target in view. A module, not a requirement.

04

Radar

A flat map of people outside the camera. Third parties show up as dots.

05

Presets

One layout for night raids. Another for daylight looting. Save both.

What is SCUM Hacks?

A Windows overlay for the game SCUM. It can show player ESP, loot ESP, an aimbot, and a radar. You buy a time-limited key.

How much does it cost?

Scout is $19 for 24 hours, Raider is $49 for 7 days, and Warden is $89 for 30 days. Those numbers are also on the pricing page.

Will the game allow this?

SCUM’s rules can treat third-party overlays as cheating. A ban is possible. This site does not promise that your account will be safe.
