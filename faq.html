Questions

Before the email.

What is SCUM Hacks?

A Windows overlay for SCUM, sold as a time-limited key on scumhacks.org. The modules are player ESP, loot ESP, aimbot, radar, and presets.

How much do SCUM hacks cost?

Scout is $19 for 24 hours. Raider is $49 for 7 days. Warden is $89 for 30 days. The pricing page is the source of those numbers.

Which platform is supported?

Windows. There is no macOS or console build.

What does the overlay show?

Player marks with name, distance, and health. Loot marks for guns, ammo, and medical gear. An optional aim assist. A radar of nearby players. Details are on the features page.

How is the key delivered?

By email after a paid order. This localhost preview does not take payment and does not send keys. The checkout form only stores a request in your browser.

Can I get a refund?

Unused keys can be refunded inside the window on the refunds page. A key that has already been issued is the product.

Can I be banned for using this?

Yes. SCUM can treat outside overlays as cheating. Losing the account is a real outcome. Do not buy if that risk is unacceptable.

Are you affiliated with the game studio?

No. SCUM Hacks is independent. SCUM belongs to its owners.
