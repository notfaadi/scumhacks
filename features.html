The kit

What you see on the glass.

SCUM Hacks draws on top of the game. These are the modules. Every paid key includes all of them.

Module 01

Player ESP

Other prisoners show as marks with a name, a distance, and a health read when the module has that data. Marks stay visible through cover, which is the whole point of player ESP on a map this thick with trees and half-built bases.

Use it to know who is holding a road or sitting on a roof. It does not walk your character or fire your gun.

Module 02

Loot ESP

Loot ESP highlights guns, ammunition, and medical items. Food and tools can be included when the build lists them. The aim is simple: skip the shack that has nothing you need.

SCUM’s loot is spread across bunkers, wrecks, and other people’s crates. The overlay is a filter, not a teleporter.

Module 03

Aimbot

The aimbot is optional sight assistance toward a target already in view. Leave it off if you only want information on screen. It is the same key either way.

It does not replace recoil control, range, or the decision to take the shot.

Module 04

Island radar

The radar is a flat map of nearby players who sit outside your camera. A third party coming from the coast shows up as a dot before they show up in the scope.

Player ESP is the world. The radar is the table next to it. Most people run both.

Module 05

Presets

Save a quieter layout for looting and a louder one for a fight. Switching presets keeps you from rebuilding the overlay every time the sun goes down.

Compare keys or read the longer field note.
