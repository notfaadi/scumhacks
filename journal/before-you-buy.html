Check five things, then decide.

People lose money on lookalike SCUM cheat shops. The checks below are boring on purpose.

1. The domain

This shop is scumhacks.org. If the address is a misspelling, a random subdomain, or a file host, it is not this desk.

2. The platform

The overlay is for Windows. If you play on a console or a Mac, there is nothing here to buy.

3. The price

Scout $19, Raider $49, Warden $89. If a page asks for a different number, stop and reopen pricing from the menu on this site.

4. The refund

Unused keys follow the refund page. Once a key is issued, that is the delivery. Read that before you need it.

5. The account

SCUM can treat a third-party overlay as cheating. A ban can wipe the character, the base, and the hours. If that is unacceptable, do not buy. This site will not tell you the risk is zero.

Questions after that belong on support.
