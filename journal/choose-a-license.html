Keys

Pick the days you will use.

The overlay does not change between plans. Scout, Raider, and Warden are the same kit with different clocks. Buy the clock that matches the calendar, not the one that looks serious.

Scout — 24 hours, $19

Buy Scout when you want a single raid, a friend’s server for one night, or a first look at the overlay. It is the most expensive plan per hour, and that is fine if you might not be back tomorrow.

Raider — 7 days, $49

Buy Raider when SCUM is already on the week’s schedule. A few evenings and a longer Saturday fit inside seven days. This is the plan most people should start with if they already know they like the game.

Warden — 30 days, $89

Buy Warden when the island is the main game this month. It is the cheapest per day. It is a bad deal if you are still deciding whether you want the overlay at all. Try Scout or Raider first.
