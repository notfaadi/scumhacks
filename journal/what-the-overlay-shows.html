Field note

What the overlay shows.

SCUM is a prison island with a long sightline problem. Jungle, wrecks, and half-finished bases hide people until they are close. SCUM Hacks is a Windows overlay drawn on top of that picture. It does not replace the game, and it does not play the character for you.

Player ESP

Player ESP marks other prisoners with a name, a distance, and a health read when the module has it. The marks stay up through cover. On a road, that is the difference between “the tree line looks empty” and “two names are sitting 80 meters off the shoulder.”

It is information. Your character still has to move, reload, and eat. SCUM’s body simulation does not care what is drawn on the overlay.

Loot ESP

Loot ESP calls out guns, ammunition, and medical supplies. When the build includes them, food and tools can show too. The useful part is negative space: the house with no marks is a house you can walk past.

Aimbot

The aimbot is optional sight assistance toward a target in view. Plenty of buyers leave it off and keep the ESP and the radar. It is included on every key so you are not upsold into a second product to try it.

Radar and presets

The island radar is a flat map of players outside the camera. Player ESP is what you see in the world. The radar is the table that catches the person walking in from the side you are not watching.

Presets store two layouts. A loot night and a fight night should not force you to rebuild the same panel every sunset.
